naya zamana ka karyakram

August 13, 2026 — Om Raheja

yah lekh kyun likha? 29 tarik julai ka, men so nahi paya kyonki mera sochen aur yojana galat ya mushkil lag rahe the. pas ke dost se bat karate hi aur pahad chalakar yah likhana shuru kiya.

navi munbai, navi bhasha

mere sab sochen angrezi men nahin hone vale hai. daraasal chhota umar se mujhe mer bhasha padane ka utsukata hai. mere yadon men hai lamahe jab men chhota bhim dekhata tha vijayavad men rehakar. yah tha mera pura shiksha. abhi, satara sal ke bad mera padana aur likhana kafai hogaya aur shiksha mili hai ki aur kaise badhun. hindi bhi mera rashta्r ka bhasha nahi, mujhe abhi urdu, sindhi, panjabi aur bahut sara sikhana hai. dusare rashta्ron se vartalap karana bhi zaruri hai, yani arebik, chini aur bahut sare jine nam abhi nahi janata hun. yani, main abhi ke lie hindi me likhane vala hun. pahale, mera vidyalay men angrezi padana zaruri nahin. mere sabase pahale yadon se vapas milun, parivar aur rashta्r. yah nur najar men lagata hai ki kisi bhi tariqe se mere logon ko apane sanskriti se milaun kyonki zindagi me aur hai kam se alava. upanivesh se age badhana ke lie ekadam zaruri hai aur vo nadi jisaka dusara tarafa samajavad hai ka ek pathar lagata hai zalim ka shakti mitane. daraasal, samajavad ke bina pura sal naukari men zindagi nikal jayega. naukari ke bahar shiksha bhi mahatta्vapurn hai. sab ko pasand hota hai ek sanskrit adami. usaka arth nahin ki angrezo ka sanskriti mera banane ka hai jaise main sochata tha aur bharatavasiyon sochate hai abhi, yani asali matalab hain ki apane sanskriti se vapas milana. mera bharatavasiyon zubanon ka utsukata vapas aya jab men bharat gaya yah sal ka shuruat men, havai adda men har shabd padane koshish kiya. "dhumrapan, mana, nikas, prasthan, jahaz, kripaya…” aur dhire dhire mere, mehan ke sath mere shabdo vapas aye. achanak mujhe sab kuch hindi men likhana tha, sochen, khushi, samavay ke bare men bhi. urdu to mene gane ke shabdo padhakar sikha aur urdu zubanon ke doston ko khub sara puchha. nind ke pahale hindi aksharon ka ishara ata tha, aur men padane koshish karata tha. bahut bar kohi asali shabd nahi tha lekin mera kam tha padana. likhate hue, shabdo ke ishare bhi vapas a raha hai. aur yah sab aya chini ko sikhane ke bich. japan ka bhasha bhi padane koshish kiya, kyonki angrez zalim ka shakti kam karane ke kohi bhi tariqe ka istemal karana parega. vapas chini sikhunga, lekin yah bhi mahatvapurn hai. failahal, mera kam men khush hun. hasin ka bat hai ki aisaland akar mujhe yah gyan vapas mila. mere lekh hindustani men hone vale hai jab tak mujhe jyada urdu nahin aye. aur abhi men likhana shuru kiya, pata nahin mujhe suryoday aur suryast ke kiranan nur lagate.

dusaron se sochen mitana

yah faaisala lene ke kabil bahut sare log khush nahi hai. jo hindi bolate hai, unhon ko bhi angrezi mahatta्vapurn baten ke lie chahie, unake namaskar kagazon ke jaise. dusare logon ko sanskriti ka muly samaj me ata hai, lekin hindi men likhana aur sochen mitana ka arth abhi nahin samaj me ata hai. sach bataun to pura arth mujhe abi bhi samajhane namumakin hai, lekin zindagi men age barate hue muly apane ap mere samane kharaa ho jaega. aj ka zamane men logon ko angrezi aur angrez aksharon zyada ata hai. aur kyonki mujhe puri tarah se urdu likhana mushkil hai abhi ke lie, mera farz hai ki yah lekh vo bhasha aur aksharon men mil sakata hai. lekin, main hindi me likh sakata hun, aur vebasait men apane ap dusara bhasha kar sakata hun. yah tariqe se men apana sanskriti ko bada sakata hun. mere sochen ko shayad sab jano se chupane me faaida hai, lekin aur sochana parega. torae doston kabhi bhi nahin janane vale hai mera hindi men likhane ka tariqa. yani, dudusaron bhashaon bhi padana parega, isame naya kuch nahin hai.

ichchhe

abhi saval uthata hai ki main age jakar kya karunga. naukari aur zindagi men santulan zaruri hai. sabase mahatta्vapurn hai samajavad ka kam. janata dal aur naukari ke kam dono bahut sara samay lenge. naukari (yani mera shiksha) zaruri hai kyonki vyapari banana chahata hun. punjipati nahin, keval ek paisa kamane ka tariqa jisase mera parivar hindustan men reh sakate hai. mma davaion ka shiksha karana chahati hai, lekin idhar kam karana mushkil hai aur bharat men itana paisa nahi milega jaise agar madad karana logon ko, mere mamaji ko, ya aram mile aur aisaland jaise jaga dekhana paye. to, mere vyapar men shiksha ka kam karunga, jaise davaion ka ya takaniki aiai se ya khilaune kaise sikhana taqi achchhe se sikhe. isake alava, vittiy ka vyapar jo aiai se apane ap vittiy ko kharidega aur bechega. aisa vyapar ke karanan jidhar se bhi kam karana chahate hai kar sakate, aur dusare man ke ichchhe ka kam bhi kar sakate hai. mere lie asali kam hoga janata dal men. janata ka ladai men, samajavad ke kitabon men, logon aur unake rashta्ron ke vartalap men. dusara hai ki apane parivar aur doston ke lie samay ho, kyonki zindagi ek bar ata hai. gana, nachana, goshthi aur kriket, fautabol aur dusare chizon maza ke lie. mera parivar aur sanskriti se jodane bharat jana kafai hai. main itana sara samajavad ka bat kar raha hun. mere pas bahut doston hai jinako amir banana hai ya bade ghar banane chahate hai, lekin isaka arth nahi hai ki ham doston nahin rah sakate hai. jaise ham samajavad ke pas ponchhenge, mera vyapar ka kam, shahar se dudur men ghar aur paise aur paise kamane ka faaida kam hote jayega. vaise, paise matalab shakti , aur shakti ka zaruri hai. logon ko dudur hatane ka zarurat nahin hai, agar punjipati nah ban jaye. punjipati se dosti ka faaida age jakar dekhenge. socho jo kar rahe hai apane bhalai ke lie kar rahe hai aur apana kam par dhyan rakho.

bijali ka abhiyanta kyun

abhi, puchhana chahie maine bijali ka abhiyanta padane kyun chuna. nahi samajavad ka kamayab ke lie zaruri hai, nahi mera vyapar men sikhane vala hun (aur men itane paise kyun kharcha karunga, agar men takaniki sikha sakata hun). samajho ki men vittiy ka padhai karunga kamai ke lie. naukari kaise ponchhana sikhaenge aur mujhe vyapar karana hai. shayad yah naukari karane men paisa achchha milata hai. ghante zyade bhi hote hai. vaise bhi mujhe nahin pata ki punjipati ko zyada kamayab banane men samajavad ko toda bhi madad karega. sangh banana bhi mushkil hoga agar naukari men gaya aur vyapar kam nahin kiya. vyapar banana kyun padhun agar vyapari ka shiksha balki vidyalay ke bahar hota hai. vipananan ko shayad faaida ho lekin bahar se khub sare moka milenge, keval vartalap sikhana padhega. naukari men shayad faaida ho, lekin jisake karanan men vittiy nahin padhunga, mai yah nahin padhunga. to mujhe laga ki asali chizon banana aur madad karana ka farz pura karane ke lie bijali abhiyanta ka shiksha karunga. yah zamane men, paise ya vishv vidyalay ke bina (ya koi achchha guru) yah padana asanbhav hai. lagata hai ki kam ayega achchha takaniki karane ya koi achchha naukari jidhar sangh bana sakata hun ya dudur se kam kar sakata hun aur samajavad men bhi faaida hai. sach bataun to agar men chahata tha ki vo chizon padhun to kar sakata tha, lekin mujhe aisa laga ki kal ka zamane men bijali abhiyanta men zyada upayog hai. kyonki men yah mera vishv vidyalay ka lekh men nahin likh sakata hun, mera lekh itana achchha nahin tha.

klab aur vyasang

bijali abhiyanta aur mere sab ichchhe aur farz pura karane ja raha hun mera vishv vidyalay. bahut sare klab men rahana, unake logon se vartalap karana aur zindagi ka shiksha pura karane karane ke lie zaruri hai. aise klab men jana hai jidhar

duniya barane ke tariqe aur janata ke bich rahana sikhana zaruri hai. vakt ate hi pata lagega in mese kain se karunga aur

dusare chizon jo abhi pata bhi nahin hai. thode klab, jaise chitr ya kitabon ka klab men lagata hai jane ka faaida nahin hai, lekin logon se milane men shayad faaida hoga. agar mujhe vo klab men utsukata nahin hai to shayad nahin jaunga. isaka matalab nahin hai ki mujhe chitr banakar ya kitabon padane pasand nahi hai, keval klab men muly shayad nahi hai.

vishvavidyalay

guru se bat karana, anubhav karana, naukari dhundhana, yah sab mere shiksha ka kamayab ke lie zaruri hai. isake alava, 3 sal men khaatam karana itana mushkil nahi hona chahie, vaise bhi 3 sal padhai kar ke paisa bahut bach jayega aur kafai hai maza aur shiksha ke lie. mera vishv vidyalay men hindi bolane vale bahut hai. mera dost ka kamare ke bahar "jai maharashta्r" alag aur naya hoga lekin pura hosh se yah jazba karana hai. vartalap karana sikhana hai, jo logon ko nahi janata hun unako puchhana hai kidhar se aye ho, kya karate ho aur dusare savalon. sundari se bat karana bhi karana hai vartalap sikhane ke lie, lekin sab ko didi samajhane ka hai. sirpha़ sikhane ke lie baten karana hai.

garmi ka mausam

har sal yah mausam ata hai aur mujhe lagata hai ki mujhe aur karana chahie tha. sal ke dauran bhi lagata hai ki aur karana chahie tha. isake do arth hai, pahale samajhana kamayabi kya hota hai, aur kya galat kar raha hun kamayabi ka rasta men. yah bhi ho sakata hai ki main jitana nahin likhata hun mere naye gyan ke bare jitana likhana chahie. kamayab ke sochate hi, lagata hai ki paise hona, kuch vyapar ya bahut achchha naukari jisase bahut kuch sikh sakata hun aur upayog ke ya achchhe log. kyonki samajavad bahut dudur lagata hai, usaka kam mujhe itana kamayab nahi lagata hai. lekin, isako badalana chahie. yah bhi ho sakata hai ki kuch achchhe doston mile, lekin achchha ka arth kya hai mujhe nahin pata, yah bar mujhe gana bajane vale doston mile to lagata hai thoda "kamayabi" mila. agar mehan ka fal nahin milata hai, yani vyapar thoda bara ho jaye, ya achchha gana baja paye, ya kuch naya sikh jaun to lagata hai ki fal nahin mila. mujhe lagata hai ki mera kamayab se pahale sabase bara samasya hai ki zyada logon nahi janata hun jo mere kamayabi men madad kar sakate hai. jo mauke mujhe mile usake puri tarah istemal karana chahie taqi vasul ho jaye. sabase mahatvapurn hai ki main fairase subah jaladi uthana shuru karunan. agar sare nau baje ke asapas so jaun, to subah ke sare char baje ya panch baje asani se uth sakata hun. rat men sona mushkil hai kyonki sab deri se kam karate hai. khana der se banata hai, doston der tak rukate hai, aur chalan chitr ka khel bhi der se hota hai. isaka arth hai ki mujhe sochana parega kisaka mehanat karana hai aur kyun karana hai sone se pahale. doston ko bolana parega ki din men masti kar sakate hai, vaise bhi abhi ke lie din ke dauran kuch karate nahin. agar khana banane sath baje men mera madad zaruri hai, to khana banane aur sikhunga aur ghar ka kam bhi kam ho jayenge. mera takaniki sikhane vala chitr khal ko khatam karana parega. usako vipananan instagram par vipananan karana parega. jo do bachche ko abhi sikha raha hun, un do ka shiksha khatam karana parega vishv vidyalay ke jane se pahale. jo kam hai aur jo chal raha hai vo sab ka samay ek jaga men likhana chahie taqi kuch bhul na jaye. vyapar ke sangh bhi jana chahie aur kam karane ke lie ghar ke bahar jana chahie. padhai khub sara karana parega aur pata karana parega ki vishv vidyalay ponchhakar kya hone vala hai. roj kuch na kuch hai. isake alava agar vyapar par thoda kam karunan to shayad kafai hai. subah uthakar jim jana shuru karana parega.
